I have spent thirty two years watching the snoring industry sell guarantees that fall apart on contact.

Mouthguard companies offer thirty-day refunds and require the patient to ship the device back at their own cost. CPAP suppliers offer trial periods that quietly exclude the mask, the tubing, and the humidifier from the refund. Online sprays advertise money-back guarantees and route the customer through three layers of customer service designed to talk them out of using it.

The patient who has been failed by these guarantees has learned not to trust them. By the time they sit across from me they have stopped reading the fine print, because they have learned that the fine print is the entire point.

Last month the company that manufactures SnoreStop, a formula I have been recommending to my patients for over twenty years, did something I have not seen anyone in this field do before.

They simplified.

What They Did

The new guarantee, in full, is one paragraph long. It reads as follows.

That is it. No return shipping. No restocking fee. No survey to complete. No phone call to navigate. The patient emails the company, the refund is processed within one business day, and the bottle stays wherever they put it.

I want to be specific about why this matters clinically.

Why This Matters For Patients

The single biggest reason chronic snorers fail to address their condition is not cost. It is friction. Every previous solution they have tried came with a process. Sleep studies. Specialist referrals. Custom fittings. Insurance pre-authorization. Devices to clean. Machines to set up.

By the time a patient has tried two or three of these and failed, they associate the act of attempting to fix their snoring with a logistical burden that produces no result. They stop trying.

The keep-it guarantee removes the last piece of friction. The patient does not have to commit to a process. They do not have to make a decision they might regret. They order, they use the spray for thirty nights, and if it does not work, the money is back in their account and the bottle is in the bathroom.

I have watched, over the past several weeks since this guarantee was implemented, patients who had refused to try anything new for years finally agree to try this. The reason they cite is the same in nearly every case. There is nothing to lose.

Why The Company Can Afford It

I asked the company directly how the math works on a guarantee like this. The answer was straightforward.

SnoreStop has been on the market for thirty one years. The manufacturer has, by their account, refund-rate data going back to 1995. The substantial majority of patients who use the product as directed for thirty nights see their snoring stop or reduce significantly. The minority who request refunds is small enough that absorbing the cost of the bottle does not break the business model.

This is not a marketing flourish. It is the natural conclusion of a company that has been quietly producing the same formula for three decades and knows, from data, that the product earns its own guarantee.
