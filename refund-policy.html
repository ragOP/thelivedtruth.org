Refund Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

The Lived Truth is an advertorial publisher. Most purchases are completed on third-party merchant websites. Refund eligibility is determined by the merchant you buy from.

1. Third-Party Orders

Orders placed through links on this site are processed by partner merchants. We do not directly process payment for those transactions unless clearly stated at checkout.

2. Merchant Refund Window

Many partner products include a refund window (for example, 30 days), but specific terms vary by brand and offer page. Please review the merchant's policy shown at checkout.

3. How to Request a Refund

  • Locate your order confirmation email from the merchant.
  • Contact the merchant's support team directly using their official support details.
  • Include your order number, purchase date, and the reason for your request.

4. If You Need Help Finding the Merchant

If you are unsure where your order was placed, contact us and we will do our best to help identify the partner based on available order details.

5. Non-Refundable Cases

Refund approval is at the merchant's discretion and may be denied if requests fall outside the policy window or do not meet the merchant's return conditions.

6. Contact for Assistance

For help locating merchant information, email support@thelivedtruth.org.
